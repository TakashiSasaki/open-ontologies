プロパティと値

プロパティ値
typeAnnotationProperty
label"acronym"
subPropertyOfabbreviation
definition"abbreviation that is made up of the initial letters of the components of the full form of a term or proper name or from syllables of the full form"
example"Examples of acronyms are: laser, ISO, GATT, UNESCO, UNICEF"
note"Acronyms are frequently pronounced syllabically. It may be useful to use this property for generation of acronym lists."
adaptedFrom"ISO 1087 Terminology work and terminology science - Vocabulary, Second edition, 2019-09"
usageNote"Note that any SPARQL or other query using entailment for the rdfs:label on any construct will return not only the rdfs:label but all labels that are specified using subproperties of rdfs:label, such as acronym."
