During this transition period, both the old Leone and new Leone will be in physical circulation for at least 90 days. The Bank of Sierra Leone will adopt the new code in the local system but SLL/694 shall remain in use until further notice. The Sierra Leonean currency shall continue to be the LEONE and this will not change after redenomination."@en
