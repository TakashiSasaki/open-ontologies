Transition loans align with emerging Climate Transition Finance principles (developed by groups such as the International Capital Market Association, ICMA). Borrowers are expected to demonstrate that the loan aligns with long-term, science-based climate goals, such as those outlined in the Paris Agreement (e.g., limiting global warming to well below 2°C)."@en
