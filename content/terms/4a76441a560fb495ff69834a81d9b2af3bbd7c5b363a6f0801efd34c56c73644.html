explanatoryNote"Funds that meet the requirements of exemption 3(c)(7) are not required to register with the SEC or provide ongoing disclosure. They are also exempt from issuing a prospectus that would outline investment positions. To qualify for the exemption, a private investment company must demonstrate that it has no plans of making an initial public offering (IPO), and its investors must be qualified purchasers. Qualified purchasers include individuals or family-owned businesses with at least $5 million in investments, trusts managed by qualified purchasers, and entities solely owned by qualified purchasers."
