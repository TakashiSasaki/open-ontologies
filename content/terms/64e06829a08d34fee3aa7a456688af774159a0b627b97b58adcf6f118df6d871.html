プロパティ値
typeClass
label"exotic warrant"@en
subClassOfWarrant
definition"warrant that gives the holder the right (but not the obligation) to buy (or sometimes sell) an underlying asset under non-standard or complex conditions, often involving additional features not found in a plain vanilla (standard) warrant"@en
explanatoryNote"Exotic warrants may be created by investment banks as part of structured products to meet specific investor needs. They are often identified with an 'X' in their English stock short name."@en
explanatoryNote"Features of an exotic warrant may include (1) non-standard payoffs, such as path-dependent or condition-based payouts, (2) variations in the underlying asset(s), which may include equities, indices, currencies, interest rates, baskets of assets, etc., and (3) embedded optionality in terms of features such as barriers, lookbacks, digitals, or dual-currency terms."@en
